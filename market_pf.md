# Property Finder: market & business

Collected 2026-11-21 from public news and PF pages. Numbers are approximate; sources differ.

## Company
- Founded 2007 (Dubai). Founder/CEO: Michael Lahyani.
- Markets: UAE (core), Qatar, Bahrain, Egypt, Saudi Arabia; stake in Zingat (Turkey).
- Acquisitions: JustProperty.com (UAE) and Bahrain Property World (2019).
- Funding:
  - Sept 2025: **$525M** led by Permira, with Blackstone; General Atlantic stays a significant minority holder. Reported valuation **> $2B**. Goals: Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Qatar expansion and AI.
  - 2024: $90M debt financing.
  - Later reports (Ares $250M; Mubadala/BECO early 2026) are **unconfirmed**.
- Revenue (UAE real estate): ~$30M (2021) -> **$117M (2024)**; $73M in H1 2025. Group revenue CAGR 40%+ (2020-2024).
- Scale: several million monthly visits across markets (exact numbers not public).

## Business model (who pays)
- **Agencies / brokers**: main revenue. Annual contracts with **credits**; listings cost credits by value/market; paid upgrades (Featured, Premium, Spotlight) for ranking and exposure.
- **Developers**: new-project pages, launches, lead generation for off-plan sales.
- **Mortgages**: lead generation / brokerage for banks (pre-approval, callbacks).
- **Data products**: transaction and valuation data (DataGuru), market reports.
- Consumers use it for free. Value for payers = **qualified leads** (calls, WhatsApp, emails).

## Competitors
| Competitor | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Bayut** (Dubizzle Group) | #1 rival in UAE. Dubizzle Group adj. revenue ~$183M (2024). AI: BayutGPT, AI Search, Bayut app inside ChatGPT. Reported IPO plans. |
| **Dubizzle** | Classifieds incl. property, same group as Bayut. |
| Propsearch, Houza, agency sites | Smaller. |
| Developer direct sales, WhatsApp/Instagram agents | Indirect competition for leads. |
- PF self-reports share of sale-listing impressions on its platform: 47% (2024) -> 49% (2025).
- AI race: Bayut moved early on conversational search; PF answers with **Scout**, Home Valuation (6-month forecast), Credit Optimizer for agents.

## UAE / Dubai real estate market
- **Dubai 2025 (DLD)**: 270,328 transactions (+20% YoY), AED **917B** total value (+20%). Sales only: ~215K deals, AED ~682B. Record year.
- **Off-plan** ~63-65% of transactions (53% of value). Developers dominate (Emaar, DAMAC, Sobha, Binghatti, Nakheel, Aldar).
- **Rents**: +~17% in 2025 (CBRE); 2026 forecasts range from -5% to +6%. Market is cooling and becoming more tenant-friendly. Q1 2026: apartment rents +2% QoQ, villas flat.
- **Supply**: large deliveries 2026-2027 in mid-market areas (JVC, JVT, Dubailand, Dubai South) -> oversupply risk, vacancy may reach ~12%.
- **Prime areas** stay strong: Downtown, Palm Jumeirah, Dubai Marina.
- **Demand drivers**: population growth (expats, HNWIs relocating), Golden Visa, no income tax, safe-haven investment, Expo City / Al Maktoum airport growth (Dubai South).
- UAE proptech market ~$0.7B (2025, MarkNtel estimate).

## Clients & audience
- ~90% of UAE residents are expats; many languages (English, Arabic, Hindi/Urdu, Russian, Chinese...). Scout's "search in the language you think in" targets this.
- Large international investor share (buy remotely, off-plan).
- Renters: most residents rent; annual contracts paid in 1-4 cheques (pain point; "Rent in 12" solves it).
- Agents: tens of thousands in Dubai, high turnover, compete on speed and trust; very WhatsApp-driven.

## Growth opportunities
1. **AI-first search & advice** (Scout): move from filters to conversation; investment advice; multilingual.
2. **Saudi Arabia** (Vision 2030, Riyadh growth), Egypt, Qatar expansion.
3. **Transactions & fintech**: Rent in 12 (monthly rent), mortgages, upfront-cost estimates, maybe deposits/payments.
4. **Data monetization**: valuations, forecasts, DLD-based insights for investors and agencies.
5. **Agent productivity SaaS**: AI lead scoring, auto listing quality, CRM, credit optimization.
6. **Developers / off-plan**: better launch discovery, payment-plan comparison, investor matching.
7. **Trust**: verified listings, fewer fake listings, agent reviews.

## Risks
- Market cycle: cooling rents and possible oversupply reduce agency budgets.
- Bayut competition, especially in AI.
- Dependence on agencies' spending; developers going direct.
- Regulation (RERA, DLD rules on listings and ads).
