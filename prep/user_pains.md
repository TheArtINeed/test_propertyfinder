# Big user pains on a UAE property portal (sourced)

Collected 2026-11-21 from web search; key pages opened and checked the same day. Article dates are given because several sources are old. Items still marked **verify** could not be opened (404, 403 or unreadable PDF).

These pains are assumptions from open sources and hands-on experience at another property portal, not Property Finder internal data.

## 1. Fake, unavailable and duplicate listings
- Regulator view: Trakheesi permit required for every Dubai ad. On 14 Feb 2024 DLD gave agents 3 working days to update portals and remove unavailable units; the same article reports 30 firms fined AED 50K each for breaking advertising terms (checked). [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/property/dubai-3-day-deadline-to-remove-fake-property-listings-on-online-platforms-real-estate-agents-tol), [Arabian Business](https://www.arabianbusiness.com/industries/real-estate/dubai-real-estate-fake-listing-deadline-agents-must-remove-fraudulent-ads-or-face-fines)
- Still a problem with "verified" badges (Gulf News, updated Jul 2026, checked): a realtor asks portals to cross-check the badge against the permit, as some ads look verified with another property's permit. No portal is named, no numbers. [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/property/dubai-property-portals-urged-to-crack-down-on-misleading-verified-listings-1.500593234)
- Users (Khaleej Times reader poll, Feb 2024, >2,800 responses, not a representative survey; checked): 32.4% saw fake photos, 21.5% were lured by unavailable or taken listings. [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/property/dubai-3-day-deadline-to-remove-fake-property-listings-on-online-platforms-real-estate-agents-tol). Bayut survey (2020): >33% named fake listings the biggest challenge in online search (**verify**, not opened).
- Duplicates: one unit may be marketed by up to 3 brokers under Form A, and sold units must be removed (rules from Oct 2022, described in a broker's opinion piece, checked; not confirmed on a DLD page, may have changed in 2024), but more copies appear and sold units stay online. [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/analysis/dubais-rules-on-property-listings-will-easily-slot-into-place-1.1665133489048), [Gulf News: tenant search](https://gulfnews.com/business/property/from-fake-listings-to-colluding-agents--a-dubai-tenants-search-for-a-rental-property-1.63388486)
- **PropWorth angle:** the verified owner is the source of truth for a unit's status (for rent / for sale / not available) and for which brokers hold a Form A.

## 2. Owners harassed by agent cold calls
- DLD bans brokers from obtaining owner data and from cold calling (PDF could not be read, **verify**; the ban is confirmed by press below). [DLD regulation (PDF)](https://dubailand.gov.ae/media/15lfelxh/regulations_governing_communication_with_property_owners_and_the_prohibition_of_cold_callings.pdf)
- Enforcement (Apr 2022, checked): 9 brokers suspended for 3 months, their firm fined AED 50K. [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/property/cold-calling-costs-nine-property-brokers-in-dubai-with-3-month-suspension-dh50000-fine-for-firm-1.1651229004619)
- Owner data is traded (Aug 2024, checked): one anonymous agent says owner and HNWI data sells for AED 3K-8K; not verified by the paper. Telemarketing rules from 27 Aug 2024: fines up to AED 150K, calls 9am-6pm. [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/uae/2024/08/15/how-uae-cold-callers-snare-private-data-on-sale-to-the-highest-bidder)
- Government answer (May 2022, checked): "Report Malicious Calls" e-service at DLD; owners who want offers opt in to the **Green List** in the Dubai REST app (launched Aug 2020): choose buy/sell/lease, property type, area, budget; opt out any time. [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/living-in-uae/housing/dubai-want-to-stop-cold-calls-from-real-estate-agents-here-is-how-you-can-report-it-online-1.1651844456041)
- **PropWorth angle:** owner states intent (not selling / open to offers from X / rent from date); agents reach the owner only through PF. Must fit with, not copy, the DLD Green List.

## 3. Bank valuation below the agreed price (resale buyers)
- Banks apply LTV to the lower of price and bank valuation; the buyer pays the gap in cash or renegotiates. Seen in several broker guides only; the guide page returned 404 and the Central Bank rulebook 403 (**verify**). [FAM Properties](https://famproperties.com/dubai-real-estate-guides/bank-valuation-final-approval-dubai), [UAE Central Bank rulebook](https://rulebook.centralbank.ae/node/2850)
- Real case: buyer asks how to cancel MOU and get deposit back after a low valuation. [LegalAdviceME](https://legaladviceme.com/questions/88373/uae-canceling-mou-getting-deposit-back-low-bank-valuation-overpriced-property)
- **PropWorth angle:** "likely bank valuation" on the listing before the offer; the same estimate continues as the owner's value after the deal.

## 4. Landlord-tenant disputes
- Common RDC cases per an RDC judge (Oct 2025, checked): eviction for non-payment, rent renewals, wrongful eviction claims. [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/dubai-tenant-landlord-disputes)
- Scale (Khaleej Times, 10 Oct 2026, checked): 2,350 amicable settlements worth AED 401M in Jan-Sep 2026, six days on average (settled cases only). Q2 2025: 443 settlements, AED 190.7M (RDC, not opened). [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/legal/dubai-rental-disputes-settled-six-days-average-2026), [RDC](https://rdc.gov.ae/en/news-events/dubai-rental-disputes-center-concludes-443-settlement-agreements-worth-aed-1907-million-in-q2-2025/)
- **PropWorth angle:** renewal in one place for both sides: allowed increase per RERA index, 90-day notice, Ejari, Rent in 12.

## 5. Service charges
- Owners dispute charges; RERA approves budgets via Mollak; the DLD Service Charge Index is the benchmark but weakly enforced. [The National 2026](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/money/2026/09/26/uae-property-are-you-paying-too-much-in-service-charges-for-your-apartment/), [DLD index](https://dubailand.gov.ae/en/eservices/service-charge-index-overview/?r=1), [CMS](https://cms.law/en/are/legal-updates/service-charges-a-tug-of-war-between-management-companies-and-property-owners)
- **PropWorth angle:** "your service charge vs the index and vs similar buildings" as part of cost of ownership.

## 6. Upfront costs for renters
- 5% deposit (10% furnished), ~5% agency fee, 1-4 cheques; deposit deductions at move-out. No survey found on how often deposits are withheld. [PF guide](https://www.propertyfinder.ae/blog/look-out-for-the-6-hidden-costs-of-renting-in-dubai), [Bayut](https://www.bayut.com/mybayut/hidden-costs-renting-dubai/), [Business Today on a Reddit thread](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/latest/world/story/landlords-hold-all-the-power-reddit-post-calls-dubai-rental-market-a-frustrating-maze-with-minimal-tenant-protection-hidden-fees-468705-2025-03-20)
