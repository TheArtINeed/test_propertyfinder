# PropWorth "My Home": product features

Author: Olga Degtiareva. Prepared for the Property Finder Product Builder challenge.

Problem: PropWorth is used once. An owner checks the value and has no reason to come back. Each feature below gives owners, renters or buyers a reason to return, or turns a visit into a lead. Pilot: one JVC building (Luma Park Views), real DLD and Ejari data, 1 Jan – 20 Nov 2026.

## Owner

| Feature | What it does | Why |
|---|---|---|
| PropWorth valuation | Value with a P25–P75 range, confidence, the real deals behind it, 12-month outlook, price chart building vs JVC | The entry point owners already use. Showing the deals makes the number credible. |
| "What this means" | One plain reading with one action per number: price vs JVC, momentum, liquidity, yield, letting wave | Owners want to know what to do, not read statistics. |
| Monthly-update preview | On the valuation: a real top deal of the month, a building news item with its price effect, the lease reminder | Shows the value of subscribing at the moment of choice. |
| "Get this every month" | Save the home: let or live, rent and lease end, price paid and loan; channel (WhatsApp, email, in-app) and frequency | Turns a one-off check into a monthly habit through the channel owners actually read. |
| My Home monthly update | Rents, deals and demand in the building, capital and yield, one next step (e.g. "lease ends in 90 days: re-let or renew") | The reason to open PF every month. One next step instead of a dashboard. |
| PropWorth portfolio summary | For owners with several units: total value, rent in, gross yield and the one home that needs attention | Investors own more than one unit; one view keeps all of them on PF. |
| News on your home | Building and area news, each item with its effect on your price ("supports your estimate", "view risk"). From DLD and Ejari: record deals, busy months, new leases. Labelled examples: a planned tower next door, a new school, road works, service charges | Something new each visit, even when the price does not move. Owners care about what changes the value of their home. |
| Owners' community | A space for verified owners of the building only, no agents and no ads: posts by topic, "Helpful" reactions, replies. Posting opens after ownership is verified. Posts are illustrative | Owners come back to talk to neighbours (service charges, tenants, repairs), not only to check a number. Verification keeps it trusted. |
| Plans card | "Not now / rent out from / open to offers from" with a price and a date | Captures intent months before the owner lists. |
| Agent request with status | Who gets the request, status Sent → Accepted → Viewing | A qualified lead for agents (PF revenue); the owner sees it is handled. |
| Privacy controls | Consent, remove a home, delete all data | Owners share sensitive data only if they can take it back. |

## Renter

| Feature | What it does | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Rent watch | Your rent vs new leases in the building or JVC, from Ejari | Answers the renewal question renters really have. |
| Allowed increase | RERA bands, 90-day notice, link to the Rental Dispute Centre | Gives the renter a fact-based position with the landlord. |
| Renewal reminders | 90 and 30 days before lease end | Brings renters back at the moment they decide to stay or move. |
| Owner-confirmed units | Units whose owners plan to let around your lease end (±30 days), with a viewing request | Links the renter side to owners' plans; inventory no other portal has. |
| Rent vs buy | Rent against a mortgage with an editable rate, link to the buyer check | Moves a renter toward buying on PF. |

## Buyer

| Feature | What it does | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Fair price check | Asking price vs DLD resales, negotiation advice | Buyers trust a portal that tells them a price is too high. |
| Bank valuation gap | Gap at 80% LTV if the bank values below the price | The buyer's main fear before an offer. |
| Mortgage pre-approval and "ask an agent" | Next steps after the check | Turns a check into a mortgage or agent lead. |
| "I bought this" | Opens the valuation prefilled with the price paid | Today's buyer becomes tomorrow's owner on PF, with no re-entry. |

## Agent (outside the prototype, receives the leads)

The agent side is not built. Agents appear only where a lead reaches them:
- **Owner:** plans "rent out from" or "open to offers from" with a price and date become a qualified lead.
- **Renter:** a viewing request for an owner-confirmed unit.
- **Buyer:** "ask an agent" after the price check.

The owner sees who gets the request (an illustrative top agent in the building) and its status: Sent → Accepted → Viewing. Nothing is sent to real agents.

Why: PF earns from agents. A lead that comes with the owner's price, date and home details is worth more than a cold enquiry. Owners and renters see the request is handled, so they trust the next one.

## Across the product

| Feature | Why |
|---|---|
| Source label on every number (DLD, Ejari, illustrative) | Trust: users see what is real and what is an example. |
| Empty and low-data states with a next action | No dead ends. |
| Entry points on existing PF screens (listing, transactions, account, rent search, mortgage calculator) | Users reach My Home from where they already are, without new marketing. |
| Events on every key step | The 1% test can be measured from day one. |
